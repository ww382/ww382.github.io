I am a Research Assistant Professor at the University of Pennsylvania and the Children's Hospital of Philadelphia. My research interests lie in developing computational models to elucidate the biomechanical mechanisms underlying congenital and acquired cardiovascular disease. My work focuses on integrating computational mechanics with scientific machine learning to create personalized, predictive, and noninvasive computational frameworks that inform clinical decision-making in cardiovascular care.

I am looking for motivated undergraduate students, graduate students, and postdoctoral fellows to join our research efforts. Individuals with a passion for advancing personalized cardiovascular modeling and a strong background in programming, computational mechanics, and/or machine learning are encouraged to contact me with a CV.
