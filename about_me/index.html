I am a Research Assistant Professor at the University of Pennsylvania and the Children's Hospital of Philadelphia. My research combines computational mechanics with machine learning to understand the biomechanics of congenital and acquired cardiovascular disease. The objective of my team is to build personalized and predictive computational approaches that support clinical decision-making in cardiovascular care.

I'm looking for motivated undergraduate students, graduate students, and postdoctoral fellows to join our research. If you're passionate about cardiovascular modeling and have a background in programming, computational mechanics, or machine learning, please reach out with your CV.
